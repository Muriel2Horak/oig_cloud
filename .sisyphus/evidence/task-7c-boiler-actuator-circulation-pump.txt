1. PUMP FOLLOWER BEHAVIOR
-------------------------
- BoilerActuator.async_apply_plan() no longer schedules independent pump windows.
- Pump follows active heating actuation via _make_heater_callbacks() injected into

- Pump unavailability emits circulation_pump_unavailable reason code without blocking heating.
