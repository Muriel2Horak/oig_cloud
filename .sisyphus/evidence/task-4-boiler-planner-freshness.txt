Task 4 Evidence: Freshness Validation
======================================
Date: 2026-04-25

Function: validate_freshness(planner_input, slot_minutes=15, now=None)

Rule: Inputs are fresh only when current slot and next two planning slots are covered.

Price freshness:
- spot_prices must contain keys for: current_slot, current_slot+15min, current_slot+30min
- Missing any of the three slots → INPUT_STALE_PRICE
- Non-datetime keys in spot_prices → treated as stale (INPUT_STALE_PRICE)

PV freshness:
- overflow_windows must cover: current_slot, current_slot+15min, current_slot+30min
- Missing coverage for any slot → INPUT_STALE_PV
- Non-datetime window boundaries → treated as stale (INPUT_STALE_PV)
